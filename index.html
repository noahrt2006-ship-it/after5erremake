Yes, within limits. Michigan law allows tint on the windshield and the front side windows only along the top, no more than 4 inches down. Side windows behind the driver and the rear window can be darker, but they can't be covered with film that's 35% or more reflective, like mirror-style silver or gold film. There's also a medical exception with a letter from a physician or optometrist.

Rules can change, so we'll make sure what we install keeps you road-legal. You can read the law yourself at MCL 257.709.
